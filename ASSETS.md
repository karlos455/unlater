Prompt: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: offline image asset for a mobile-first procrastination app activity card. Scene/backdrop: cozy modern apartment, warm lived-in details. Style/medium: premium natural lifestyle photography, slightly playful magazine editorial. Composition/framing: landscape 4:3, subject centered with room for cropping on all edges. Lighting/mood: soft late-afternoon light, optimistic and inviting. Color palette: pastel coral, lavender, lemon and teal accents. Constraints: no logos, no text, no watermark, no phone UI, no split panels.”

As fotografias de exemplo aparecem apenas na demonstração opcional. Os cartões reais usam a imagem escolhida pelo utilizador ou uma ilustração da categoria.
